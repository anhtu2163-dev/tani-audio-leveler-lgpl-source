1. OVERVIEW & USER RIGHTS UNDER THE GNU LESSER GENERAL PUBLIC LICENSE (LGPL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TaNi Audio Leveler invokes the external command-line executable "ffmpeg.exe"
(from the FFmpeg project, built as dynamic shared libraries: avcodec-63.dll,
avdevice-63.dll, avfilter-12.dll, avformat-63.dll, avutil-61.dll,
swresample-7.dll, and swscale-10.dll).

- FFmpeg Version: n9.0.2-57-gd838f9f9ae-20261115 (Commit: d838f9f9ae)
